# Era 1: Emergence of distributed-memory compilation (1980–1987)

Working notes for the era section of the paper. Everything here is either a pointer to catalog evidence or an explicitly labelled hypothesis.

**Machine driver.** Multicomputers and hypercubes (Cosmic Cube, iPSC, nCUBE), systolic arrays, Connection Machine.

**Programming abstraction.** Explicit message passing; early global-array proposals; SIMD data-parallel languages.

**Decomposition representation.** Programmer-managed; first compiler proposals for placement.

**Generated communication.** Hand-written sends/receives.

**Limitation that motivated the next era.** Programming by hand was error-prone; portability across machines was poor.

**Boundary evidence.** Cosmic Cube (1985); first distributed-memory compilation papers appear 1988.
