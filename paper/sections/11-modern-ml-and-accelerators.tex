\section{Multi-GPU, ML, Spatial, and AI-Accelerator Compilers}
\label{sec:modern}

\fact{Mesh-TensorFlow~\cite{shazeer2018meshtf}, GShard~\cite{lepikhin2020gshard}, and GSPMD~\cite{xu2021gspmd} established the device mesh and per-tensor sharding specification as the programming abstraction of accelerator pods; PartIR~\cite{alabed2025partir} separates partitioning tactics from the program and composes them on an MLIR-based IR.} \fact{Alpa~\cite{zheng2022alpa} and FlexFlow~\cite{jia2019flexflow} search the space of parallelisation strategies automatically, while GPipe, PipeDream, Megatron-LM, and ZeRO~\cite{huang2019gpipe,narayanan2019pipedream,shoeybi2019megatron,rajbhandari2020zero} are training systems whose plan generation is largely manual and which we therefore classify as bridge work.} \fact{DISTAL and SpDISTAL~\cite{yadav2022distal,yadav2022spdistal} bring distribution formats to tensor algebra; Kong et al.\ generate distributed-memory mappings for tensor computations automatically~\cite{kong2023automatic}; a shared MLIR stack gives stencil DSLs distributed-memory parallelism~\cite{bisbas2024shared}.} \fact{On wafer-scale hardware, stencil codes have been mapped onto the Cerebras engine with explicit fabric communication~\cite{rocki2020cerebras,jacquelin2022wse}.}
