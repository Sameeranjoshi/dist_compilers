\section{Communication Generation and Redistribution}
\label{sec:communication}

\fact{Koelbel derived regular communication patterns at compile time for Kali loops~\cite{koelbel1991regular}; Chatterjee et al.\ gave closed-form local address and communication-set generation for block-cyclic distributions~\cite{chatterjee1993generating}; Stichnoth, O'Hallaron, and Gross generated communication for array statements and evaluated it in a production compiler~\cite{stichnoth1994generating}.} \fact{Fortran~D's message vectorisation hoisted element messages out of loops~\cite{hiranandani1991fortrand}.} \fact{Redistribution between distributions was optimised by Ramaswamy, Simons, and Banerjee~\cite{ramaswamy1996redistribution} and dynamic partitioning by Palermo, Hodges, and Banerjee~\cite{palermo1996dynamic}.} \fact{Polyhedral compilers compute communication sets as integer sets and emit MPI calls~\cite{bondhugula2013compiling,kruse2014molly}.} \fact{SPMD partitioners insert collectives where propagated shardings disagree~\cite{xu2021gspmd,alabed2025partir}.}

\emph{Synthesis.} \infer{Communication-set generation and collective insertion solve the same problem on different representations: the former on affine index expressions, the latter on operator semantics.
