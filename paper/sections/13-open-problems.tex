\section{Open Problems}
\label{sec:open}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item \textbf{Unified ownership/layout IR.} No representation today spans HPF-style affine distributions, mapping arrays, logical regions, and mesh shardings; MLIR dialects are the current candidates. \todo{cite MLIR shard and Shardy documentation once verified.}
  \item \textbf{Dynamic and irregular partitioning.} Inspector--executor techniques and sparse tensor distribution formats remain separate lines of work.
  \item \textbf{Hierarchical heterogeneous systems.} Node--device--core hierarchies are handled by nested mappings in Sequoia and Alpa but not by a general mechanism.
  \item \textbf{Topology and memory-capacity modelling.} Cost models in SPMD partitioners seldom model network topology or capacity limits jointly.
  \item \textbf{Correctness and consistency.} Verification of generated communication is rarely addressed.
  \item \textbf{Communication/computation overlap, composability, debuggability, performance portability.}
  \item \textbf{Wafer-scale and fine-grained local-memory machines.} Spatial placement with per-PE memory re-opens the placement problem at a scale where closed-form distributions do not apply.
\end{itemize}
