\section{Open Problems}
\label{sec:open}

Each problem below follows from a generalization in Table~\ref{tab:generalizations} and names the evidence that would close it.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item \textbf{Measured versus modelled cost (G2).} \emph{Test status: partly run.} Palermo et al.\ report model estimates for 32 processors and measured transposes from 8 to 128 processors, in separate tables~\cite{palermo1996dynamic}. We did not establish that the two describe the same workload, so they are not counted as a like-for-like comparison. Until a read paper gives one, the shift from analytic to measured optimisation is an explanation, not a result~\cite{gupta1992automatic,jia2019flexflow}.
  \item \textbf{Unified ownership and layout IR (G1, G7).} \emph{Test status: not run.} Closed-form owners, declared maps and propagated shardings need different analyses. The MLIR shard dialect is the nearest candidate for a shared representation, and the sparse dialect of Vaidya et al.\ is a second~\cite{mlir2025shard,vaidya2025distributed}. The first is documentation, not a carded paper (Section~\ref{sec:modern}).
  \item \textbf{Propagation under constraints.} \emph{Test status: not run.} PartIR's authors report that propagation is blocked by reshapes and non-divisible shapes~\cite{alabed2025partir}; GSPMD's sharding completion is heuristic~\cite{xu2021gspmd}. Whether propagation is unique under stated conditions has not been tested.
  \item \textbf{Overlap of reductions.} \emph{Test status: not settled.} The coded tags put overlapped communication together with a reduction in nine read papers under one coding or the other~\cite{benkner1999compiling,kung1982systolic,hiranandani1992evaluation,rogers1994compiling,gupta1995hpf,narayanan2019pipedream,yadav2022distal,rocki2020cerebras,yelick2007titanium}. A tag pair does not show that a reduction overlaps the computation that feeds it. That needs the communication sections of the cards read for it, which has not been done.
  \item \textbf{Break-even of run-time stages.} \emph{Test status: partly run.} Saltz et al.\ report the inspector's cost as several iterations' worth and an amortised efficiency~\cite{saltz1991multiprocessor}. The CHAOS measurements compare schedule reuse with no reuse~\cite{ponnusamy1995runtime}. We did not find a read paper that compares an inspector with a fully static schedule, so the break-even point against a static schedule is not reported.
  \item \textbf{Correctness of generated communication.} \emph{Test status: partly run.} A keyword search of the cards found verification or proof in seven read papers. Allen and Kennedy prove loop transformations, not communication~\cite{allen1987automatic}. PartIR's authors report that their core-to-SPMD lowering is proved correct~\cite{alabed2025partir}. Verification of generated communication is otherwise not reported in the read set.
  \item \textbf{Wafer-scale and fine-grained memory.} \emph{Test status: run.} Both read wafer-scale compilers keep the decomposition static and one tile per processing element, and the stencil runtime supports only star-shaped patterns~\cite{stawinoga2026mlir,rocki2020cerebras}.
  \item \textbf{Productivity.} \emph{Test status: run.} Productivity is recorded as an objective for five to seven of the eleven Era~6 papers, depending on the coding, but none of the read papers measures it with users~\cite{xu2021gspmd,alabed2025partir}.
\end{itemize}
